LifeGuardian

A simple personal safety app for people living alone.

Fall Detection

LifeGuardian can detect possible falls and start an alert countdown. If the alert is not cancelled, it can send an emergency SMS with location information to your chosen contact.

Inactivity Alert

If you do not respond for a long time during active hours, LifeGuardian can start an alert and notify your emergency contact.

Manual SOS

Long press the SOS button to send an emergency SMS with your location and call your emergency contact.

Privacy First

No account required. No login. No cloud upload. Your emergency contact and settings stay on your phone.
