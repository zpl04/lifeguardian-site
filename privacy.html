Effective Date: September 25, 2026

LifeGuardian - Fall Safety SOS ("LifeGuardian", "the app", "we", or "our") is designed as a privacy-first personal safety application.

LifeGuardian does not require account registration or login and does not operate a developer cloud server for storing your personal safety data. Core safety detection and processing are performed locally on your device.

1. Information and Data Processing

LifeGuardian is designed to minimize data collection. The app does not collect personal information for advertising, behavioral tracking, or profiling.

The app does not use advertising identifiers and does not sell personal information.

2. Location Information

LifeGuardian may use your device location to provide location information in an emergency SMS sent to your selected emergency contact.

When Guardian protection is manually enabled, LifeGuardian may run an Android foreground service and perform low-frequency location maintenance. The most recent trusted location may be stored locally on your device.

LifeGuardian does not create a continuous route history and does not upload your location history to a developer-controlled server.

LifeGuardian does not request Android ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION.

Emergency location states

Depending on availability and freshness, an emergency message may contain:

  • Current location: a recent trusted location, together with a map link.
  • Last location: a recent previously trusted location, together with its original acquisition time and a map link.
  • Location unavailable: when no sufficiently recent trusted location is available, the emergency SMS is still sent without a location link.

Location availability and accuracy depend on the device, operating system, satellite visibility, Wi-Fi, cellular network conditions, Google Play services, and the surrounding environment.

Failure to obtain location information does not prevent an established, non-cancelled emergency alert from sending an SMS.

3. Motion and Sensor Data

LifeGuardian may use motion sensors such as the accelerometer and gyroscope for functions including possible fall detection.

Sensor processing is performed locally on the device. LifeGuardian does not upload raw motion sensor data to a developer-controlled server.

4. Emergency SMS

When an emergency alert is confirmed and not cancelled, LifeGuardian may send an SMS from your device through your mobile carrier to the emergency contact you selected.

The developer does not receive a copy of your emergency SMS.

Standard carrier SMS is not guaranteed to be end-to-end encrypted or encrypted in transit. SMS delivery and security depend on your mobile carrier and the telecommunications network.

5. Emergency Contact Information

The emergency contact information you enter is stored locally on your device and is used to perform safety functions such as emergency SMS and dialing.

LifeGuardian does not upload your general contacts list to a developer-controlled server.

6. Emergency Calling

After an SOS or other supported emergency workflow, LifeGuardian may open the phone dialer with your selected emergency contact number to help you place a call quickly.

7. Foreground Service, Notifications and System Settings

LifeGuardian may use Android foreground services and persistent notifications when Guardian protection is active.

The app may also guide you to Android system settings related to notifications, battery optimization, background operation, overlays, or other permissions needed for safety functions.

8. Local Storage

LifeGuardian may store information locally on your device, including:

  • App settings and preferences
  • Your selected emergency contact
  • The most recent trusted location and its original timestamp
  • Limited local alert history where applicable

Android application-data backup is disabled for LifeGuardian.

9. Subscriptions and Google Play Billing

LifeGuardian may offer subscriptions through Google Play Billing.

Payment processing is handled by Google Play. LifeGuardian does not receive or store your full credit card or bank account details.

Google's own privacy and payment policies apply to transactions processed by Google Play.

10. Third-Party Platform Services

Android and Google services may assist the device in determining location using technologies such as GPS/GNSS, Wi-Fi, cellular networks, and Google Play services.

These platform services operate under their own privacy policies.

LifeGuardian may generate a Google Maps URL from location coordinates. The coordinates are not uploaded to a developer-controlled server. If you choose to open a map link, Google Maps or your browser may process the link under its own privacy policy.

11. Data Sharing and Sale

LifeGuardian does not sell your personal information.

LifeGuardian does not operate a developer-controlled server that receives your location history, emergency contacts, motion sensor data, SMS inbox, or call logs.

Emergency SMS messages are sent directly from your device through your mobile carrier to the emergency contact you choose.

12. Data Deletion

Because LifeGuardian primarily stores app data locally, you can remove locally stored information by using available in-app controls, clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app.

LifeGuardian does not maintain a developer cloud account containing your personal safety data.

13. Security

LifeGuardian is designed to minimize external transmission of personal safety data by processing core safety functions locally on your device.

However, no device, operating system, mobile network, or communications method can be guaranteed to be completely secure or continuously available.

In particular, standard carrier SMS should not be considered end-to-end encrypted.

14. Children's Privacy

LifeGuardian is not specifically directed to children under 13.

15. Safety Disclaimer

LifeGuardian is an assistive personal safety tool and is not a replacement for professional emergency services, medical devices, caregivers, or emergency-response systems.

Fall detection, inactivity alerts, location availability, SMS delivery, and other functions may be affected by device hardware, operating-system restrictions, network availability, sensor accuracy, user settings, and environmental conditions.

LifeGuardian cannot guarantee that every emergency event will be detected or that every alert, SMS, call, or location request will succeed.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's functionality, legal requirements, or privacy practices change.

The effective date shown at the top of this page identifies the current version.
